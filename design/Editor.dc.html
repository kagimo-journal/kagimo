A long walk after the rain

The river was higher than I expected. I stood on the footbridge for a long time, not thinking about anything in particular, which is exactly what I came for.

On the way back I passed the house with the green door and the lemon tree in the window. Someone had finally repainted the railings. It is strange how much a small change can make a street feel like it belongs to somebody again.

I want to remember this feeling of having nowhere to be
