Boxes, and the last night here

Saturday 14 March · Southville, Bristol

Everything we own fits into forty-one boxes, which feels both like too many and not nearly enough. The kitchen echoes now. I keep opening cupboards out of habit and finding nothing in them.

We ate chips on the floor of the front room because the table went this afternoon. The light from the streetlamp came through the bare window the way it must have done every night for six years, and I had simply never noticed.
